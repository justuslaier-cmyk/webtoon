# WHERE THE STARS FELL — EPISODE 001
## The Sky Beyond Our Fields
**Status:** vollständiges 48-Panel-Drehbuch + visuelle Produktionsvorlage; keine veröffentlichte Episode.
**Szenenkern:** Ein ungewöhnlich neugieriger achtjähriger Junge zeichnet in der Morgendämmerung seine Welt und erträumt sich andere. Seine Mutter begegnet seiner Neugier mit trockenem Humor und stiller Zuneigung. **Mira wird erst in Episode 4 eingeführt**, damit der bestehende 352-Episoden-Plan respektiert wird.
**Bildsprache:** reduzierter handgezeichnet wirkender Soft-Cinematic-Manhwa-Stil, geerdete einfache Hintergründe, echte Panelvielfalt, ruhige Pausen, keine Copy/Paste-Gesichter. Englisch nur bei Figurenrede.
**Figuren:** Aren (blond, violette Augen; ca. 8; einfaches helles Hemd); Maela (NEUER ENTWURF: dunkles honigbraunes Haar, praktisches Hauskleid, abgearbeitete Hände; Design nicht freigegeben).
**Gestaltung:** Panels sind Erzählmomente, keine technischen Upload-Bilddateien. 6 Szenen × 8 Panels. Bilder werden nach Bearbeitung separat montiert; die Schrift ist redaktionell geprüft und wird ggf. nachgelayoutet.

## 01 — The village wakes

**P01** — Wide establishing: low sun rises over a small alien but homely valley; simple roofs and a narrow watercourse
**P02** — Closer: folded cloth awning moves slightly; the village is still
**P03** — Single foreground grass stem against a pale enormous celestial disk
**P04** — Village lane: a sleepy worker rolls a small wooden cart
**P05** — Window of a modest home half open; a hand-written sheet is pinned inside
**P06** — Inside room: crude homemade notebook lies open on a pillow
**P07** — Child hand turns a wrinkled page of tiny stars
**P08** — Aren in profile, at window, violet eyes reflecting dawn  
> **AREN (thought): I was so close last night.**

## 02 — A map of somewhere else

**P09** — Aren kneels beside window; notebook open on floor
**P10** — Close-up: uneven dots joined by messy pencil lines
**P11** — He tries to join two stars with a ruler; ruler slips  
> **AREN: Come on...**
**P12** — Pencil breaks; small lead mark across his thumb
**P13** — His annoyed face then a suppressed grin at the mistake
**P14** — He sketches a lopsided circle as a planet near stars
**P15** — He compares sketch to view outside; night stars invisible in dawn  
> **AREN: Were you over there?**
**P16** — He cups face against window and peers at pale sky

## 03 — Mother in the doorway

**P17** — Low shot of kitchen doorway; Maela’s ordinary worn slippers on floor  
> **MAELA (off): Aren?**
**P18** — Aren hides notebook instinctively beneath his elbow  
> **AREN: I’m awake!**
**P19** — Maela in doorway, dark honey-brown hair tied back, simple practical dress, patient look  
> **MAELA: That wasn’t the question.**
**P20** — Aren turns, a little embarrassed  
> **AREN: It kind of was.**
**P21** — Maela’s tired but amused smile
**P22** — She spots charcoal fingerprints on the blanket  
> **MAELA: Please tell me that’s not the good blanket.**
**P23** — Aren inspects the blanket and freezes
**P24** — Maela leans against the doorframe, chuckling  
> **MAELA: Breakfast. Before the sky eats you.**

## 04 — A small lesson in scale

**P25** — Aren runs a finger over notebook page showing crooked star pattern  
> **AREN: Mom, look.**
**P26** — Maela comes closer, bends beside him  
> **MAELA: What am I looking at?**
**P27** — Close-up of page with a large dot and tiny dots  
> **AREN: Us. And maybe the others.**
**P28** — Maela studies, trying to understand  
> **MAELA: The others?**
**P29** — Aren extends his arms wide to demonstrate distances  
> **AREN: Other places. Really far away.**
**P30** — Maela looks past him into daylight; grounded gentle expression  
> **MAELA: You’d have a long walk.**
**P31** — Aren gives her a playful skeptical look  
> **AREN: I wouldn’t walk.**
**P32** — Maela raises an eyebrow  
> **MAELA: Oh?**

## 05 — The impossible boat

**P33** — Aren takes a scrap of paper, rapidly sketches a little boat with oversized wings  
> **AREN: I’d build something.**
**P34** — Close-up of mismatched wing drawing, charmingly imperfect
**P35** — Maela taps the scribbled wing  
> **MAELA: That might need another wing.**
**P36** — Aren taps page, notices he has drawn three  
> **AREN: ...Maybe.**
**P37** — Maela laughs quietly with hand to chin
**P38** — Aren in three-quarter view, serious about the impossible  
> **AREN: I wanna know what’s out there.**
**P39** — Maela gently straightens his hair without interrupting
**P40** — Quiet image of their faces in shared window light  
> **MAELA: You will. But not before breakfast.**

## 06 — First promise to the stars

**P41** — Open kitchen doorway; Maela walks out, Aren follows hugging the notebook
**P42** — Insert: small bare feet step onto cool floor
**P43** — Aren pauses at the window before leaving the room
**P44** — He glances to pale planet visible between clouds
**P45** — Aren draws a tiny star in corner of page with one quick pencil stroke
**P46** — He closes the notebook; worn hand-stitched binding close-up
**P47** — Wide quiet: Maela waits in sunlit kitchen; small table with bowls, no ornate props  
> **MAELA: Aren!**
**P48** — Final vertical panel: Aren runs toward warm kitchen light, doorway framing him; the pale other world in window behind  
> **AREN: Coming!**
