## WEBTOON FORMAT RESEARCH (as checked 2026-11-17)
- WEBTOON CANVAS upload output is optimally <=800 px wide, <=1280 px high per image. These are **upload slice limits, not fixed panel sizes**.
- The official WEBTOON Creator Resource Handbook cites 30–60 panels for typical Originals; panel count follows story needs.
- At 800 px export width, WEBTOON recommends ~200 px separation between panels as a useful starting point. We use 105–260 based on beat; adjust during editorial review.
- Production masters should be drawn at 1600 px wide where possible, exported at 800 px; note some sourced art was generated at lower resolution, and upscaling cannot create missing line-art detail.
