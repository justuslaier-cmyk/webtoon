# Global design-continuity law · confirmed user requirement

Every recurring **character, alien species, object, location, architectural set, vehicle, weapon and costume** needs a versioned visual asset sheet before finalized comic use. No silent auto-redesigns. Approved primary image overrides prose description. Each non-approved entry in the register is deliberately marked as such.

**Character sheets:** front, profile, 3/4 view, expressions, body proportions, palette and costume sheet. **Locations:** floor plan, two elevations, establishing view, day/night lighting. **Props:** proportions, 2–3 views, wear patterns and exact story changes. **Species:** anatomy, scale, dress and texture rules.
